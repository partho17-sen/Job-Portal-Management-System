Job Portal Management System

A Job Portal Management System is an online platform where employers can publish jobs and applicants can search and apply for suitable jobs quickly and easily.

Project Objective

  • ✔ Online Job Search
  • ✔ Employer Job Posting
  • ✔ Resume Upload
  • ✔ Easy Recruitment
  • ✔ Application Tracking

About Project

The Job Portal Management System helps job seekers and employers communicate through one online platform. It reduces paperwork, saves time, and makes recruitment faster and easier.

Project Features

Job Search

Find jobs based on skills and qualifications.

Company Portal

Employers can publish job vacancies easily.

Resume Upload

Upload CV directly while applying.

Application Tracking

Track submitted applications anytime.
